# Pho65 CRM Co-Pilot — Owner Guide

Open the [CRM co-pilot](http://localhost:5050/crm) to review customer follow-ups and catering bookings. This practice version uses made-up customer information only.

## What it does

- Shows which customers who agreed to follow-ups visit regularly and which have not returned recently.
- Warns when a catering time is too busy and suggests a quieter time on the same day.
- Gives you a short weekly update and writes follow-up drafts for you to review.

## What it does not do

It never sends messages, runs a membership program, or tracks visit habits for customers who have not agreed. All messages remain drafts. Every catering booking still counts toward kitchen workload.

## One known limitation

A mistyped phone number can make a returning customer look like two different people, causing a missed follow-up.

## One next step

Ask staff to double-check the phone number when a customer agrees to follow-ups.
